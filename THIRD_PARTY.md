The thirty images in `static/assets/china2098/` are existing works by Fan Wennan (范文南), from the China 2098 series. Each file is the complete painting from its corresponding artist project, downloaded without pixel edits from the artist's ArtStation CDN (4k rendition). Original embedded signatures are preserved. These images have no license granted by this repository; website reuse permission has not been verified. Selected by the site owner for the homepage background gallery.
